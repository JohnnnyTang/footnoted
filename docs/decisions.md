| D-017 | **Dilation radius (F01.6 frozen):** the effective buffer is `segments.buffer_m ?? default(kind)` (D-007). A cell at offset (dx, dy) cells from the rasterised line is kept when (dx² + dy²)·w² ≤ b², where w is the cell width at **that row's latitude**: the exact real-valued radius, computed per row, replacing the handoff's `ceil(b ÷ w)`. On a 500 m drive at z20 it halves the overreach (11.9 m vs 22.7 m) and covers 2.2 % fewer cells. Closes S01-V002 and S01-V018; the near-pole refinement (S01-V003) stays with S01-31. Evidence: S01-13, S01-10. | Decided | owner, 2026-10-10 (standing instruction) | — |
| D-018 | **Default buffer distance (confirms D-007):** a `local` segment with `buffer_m IS NULL` reveals **100 m** around its line; a user override is clamped to **25 m – 2 km**. A `transit` leg reveals its **endpoints only** (each endpoint dilated by the local default); no corridor along the line. Stage 4 (M4) finalises the transit default and its user switch. The constants live in `footnoted_geo` (`defaultLocalBufferMeters`, `minBufferMeters`, `maxBufferMeters`, frozen in `types.dart`). Rationale: at z20 100 m is a radius of 2.6 cells at the equator and 5.2 at 60° (exact radius, D-017), enough to read as "the streets I walked" without painting whole districts; 25 m is below one cell at low latitudes, the floor at which a line still reveals; 2 km covers a "the whole town" preference. | Decided | owner, 2026-10-10 (standing instruction: follow the orchestrator's recommendations; S01-E3) | Owner prefers another default after seeing it on the phone (W5). |
