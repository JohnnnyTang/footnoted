## Measurements

**Device and method.** AVD `fn_api36` (Android 16 / API 36, x86_64, 1080 × 2400 at 420 dpi, dpr 2.625, host GPU Intel Arc 140V through the emulator GLES translator), **profile** build.
- **Data:** the stand-in, 1,033,958 z20 cells, pushed as `dataset.cells.bin`.
- **Pan:** z10, 20 s, one warm-up pass and 3 measured passes.
- **Primary metric:** SurfaceFlinger present times of the app's Flutter `SurfaceView (BLAST)` layer, polled every 0.5 s and cut to the window the app reports (CLOCK_MONOTONIC).
- **Run conditions:** the emulator and host were shared with S01-10/11/13. The emulator shows two states, "fast" (VD at ≈58 fps, ≈3 % of frames over 32 ms) and "slow" (VD at ≈52 fps, ≈16 %), whatever the fog. Only rows from the same batch are compared below. Full tables are in `evidence/S01-12/tables.md`.

**Flutter side (FrameTiming):** raster p50 was 12–14 ms in VD and 8–10 ms in TextureView. The VD raster thread composites the virtual-display texture, and that costs more. Build p50 was ≈2 ms. `moveCamera` was issued on ≈75 % of vsyncs; on the rest the previous call had not been acknowledged yet.

**What each method measured** (also in MEASURE.md §2):
- SurfaceFlinger on the BLAST layer gives the on-screen frames in both modes. It is the criterion-2 number.
- The virtual display's own layer had no frames to read.
- `gfxinfo` showed **0 frames** in VD. In TextureView it showed 2,668 frames with 45.65 % janky: the map's HWUI compositing, not what reaches the screen.
- Flutter FrameTiming gives the raster thread only.
- MapLibre's `OnDidFinishRenderingFrame` is not reachable from `maplibre_gl`.

**Platform-view modes in `maplibre_gl` 0.27.1** (`MapLibreMap.useHybridComposition`; both modes go through `initAndroidView`):

| | GLSurfaceView → Flutter Virtual Display (`false`, default) | TextureView → Flutter texture layer (`true`) |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Emulator over-32 ms share | ≈3 % (fast state) | ≈20 % (fast state) |
| Flutter raster p50 | 12–14 ms | 8–10 ms |
| Flutter widgets over the map | yes (credits and controls render fine) | yes, and the map can also be transformed or clipped |
| Known limits | Virtual Display: text input, accessibility, z-order quirks | higher composition cost; `translucentTextureSurface` only here |
| Flutter's true Hybrid Composition | not offered by the plugin | — |

**Strategy (c), in-app tile source (effort only, not built):** serve fog mask tiles from a `dart:io` `HttpServer` on `127.0.0.1` as a raster source. This needs:
- a cleartext network-security exception for localhost on Android and an ATS exception on iOS;
- an entry for `127.0.0.1` in `docs/network-hosts.md`;
- per-tile PNG encoding (a `dart:ui` `Picture.toImage`, or a pure-Dart encoder);
- cache-busting by re-adding the source, because `maplibre_gl` exposes no per-tile invalidation.

Estimate: M (2–3 days) to a measurable spike. It is the soft-fog path, and nothing above says W2 needs it.

## Out-of-contract findings

Raised here only. Nothing outside `spike/render_bench/**` was edited.

1. **JDK 21 required.** `maplibre_gl` 0.27.1 sets the Android `sourceCompatibility`/`jvmTarget` to 21, so the build fails on JDK 17 (the dev box default). CI's *Android build* job pins `java-version: "17"` (`.github/workflows/ci.yml`, orchestrator-owned). It does not build the spike today, but it will fail once `app/` depends on `maplibre_gl` (S01-33).
   - Locally I unzipped Temurin 21.0.12.1+1 to `D:\software\jdk-21.0.12.1+1`, outside the repo, and set `JAVA_HOME` per command. No global Flutter or Gradle config was changed.
   - D-010 (toolchain) may want a line for this.
2. **Proprietary transitive Android dependencies (invariant 1).** The plugin's Gradle module declares `com.google.android.gms:play-services-base:18.10.1` and `play-services-location:21.4.0`. They are used only by its high-accuracy location engine. The spike excludes them in its own `build.gradle.kts`, and the map runs (dependency count 0, screenshots).
   - `tool/check_deps.dart` reads only `pubspec.lock`, so it cannot see them.
   - `app/` needs the same exclusion in S01-33, plus a guard in S01-42.
   - Caveat: the plugin's `catch (e: Exception)` does not catch `NoClassDefFoundError`, so FootNoted must never enable the plugin's high-accuracy location engine without GMS. Recording uses its own location path anyway.
3. **Windows cross-drive Kotlin bug.** With the pub cache on `C:` and the repo on `D:`, Kotlin incremental compilation of the plugin fails (`Could not close incremental caches …`). Fix: `kotlin.incremental=false` in the Android `gradle.properties`. **W3's `app/android/gradle.properties` will hit the same bug** on this machine.
4. **Location permissions from the AAR.** The MapLibre AAR merges `ACCESS_FINE_LOCATION`/`ACCESS_COARSE_LOCATION` into the manifest. They are not background permissions, but S01-30/S01-33 should decide whether to declare them deliberately or strip them with `tools:node="remove"` until recording ships.
5. **Gradle and the proxy.** Gradle ignores the `HTTPS_PROXY` environment variable, and Maven Central TLS handshakes failed without the local proxy. Prefetching once with `-Dhttps.proxyHost/-Dhttps.proxyPort` worked (MEASURE.md §3). No repo or global file was changed.
6. **Shared emulator.** A sibling app took the foreground once mid-run, and sibling load moves the emulator between its fast and slow states. `measure.dart` now detects preemption (exit 3) and the batch script retried. Absolute emulator numbers are therefore noise-limited.
7. **The pan path is tied to the stand-in.** `lib/src/pan.dart` crosses Berlin and the Berlin→Leipzig corridor. When S01-10's dataset replaces the stand-in, its home city and road trips may be elsewhere. S01-20 must re-point `panWaypoints` at S01-10's dense city and a road-trip corridor, or the pan will cross empty fog.

## Handover

- **Recommended W2 strategy:** (a) holes at detail 6 (z16 cells at z10) on the plugin-default GLSurfaceView/Virtual Display. Reasons:
  - it is the handoff's decided design;
  - it sends fewer rectangles and bytes than (b) (274 vs 386, 34 vs 45 KB at z10);
  - on the emulator its cost was indistinguishable from no fog and from (b), even at detail 8 (1,400–1,700 rectangles);
  - the only robust emulator signal is that TextureView costs about 6× more frames over 32 ms.

  Keep (b) as the fallback if a phone shows tessellation artefacts or cost from hole-heavy polygons (adjacent holes share edges, which is technically invalid GeoJSON; MapLibre rendered it correctly here).
- **To reproduce or extend:** follow `spike/render_bench/MEASURE.md`. Build with JDK 21. `adb push` the dataset as `dataset.cells.bin`, then run `dart run bin/measure.dart … --label "<model, Android version>"` and `dart run bin/summarize.dart`. Report the worst pass.
