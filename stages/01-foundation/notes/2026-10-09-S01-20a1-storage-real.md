**Recommendation: confirm Drift (D-003), with one rule for the coverage hot path.** Drift's typed batch API costs 2.6–4.6× on the bulk coverage writes, but the same Drift connection running prepared SQL through `customStatement` batches is within 6 % of raw on the full insert. So the schema, migrations (the S01-32 snapshot harness), typed queries and streams stay on Drift, and the reveal writer (span insert + `cell_stats` upsert + delete maintenance) uses `customStatement` batches. Large z20 reads (fog at z14+) are 2.3× slower through Drift's row mapping; the fog reader should take z20 cells only for small tiles, use `cell_rollups` (≤ 0.3 ms either way) for wide views, and if needed read through the raw `sqlite3.Database` handle that Drift's `NativeDatabase` exposes in its `setup` callback. That handle was not measured here (backlog row). Not measured either: Drift's `createInBackground` isolate hop.

It is the cheaper rule (2 % fewer cells) and halves the overreach. The near-pole refinement (target-row width, S01-V003) stays with S01-31.
