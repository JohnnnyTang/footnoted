### Shared machine: the measurement lock

Both sessions share one Windows box and one emulator (`fn_api36`, started with `D:/software/android-sdk/emulator/emulator -avd fn_api36`). W1 showed that contention spoils timing numbers. The rule:

- **Before any timed run** (desktop AOT storage timings, any emulator storage or render run), take the lock with `mkdir C:/Users/12754/AppData/Local/Temp/fn-measure.lock` (atomic; it fails if the lock is held) and write your session ID and the UTC time into `holder.txt` inside it. Release it by removing that directory as soon as the batch ends. Hold it for at most 45 minutes at a time.
- **While the other session holds it**, do not start Gradle builds, AOT builds or test suites. Do non-CPU work, or wait and re-check. A lock older than 90 minutes is stale: you may remove it, and you record that in your note.
- **The emulator:** whoever needs it first starts it (no wipe, no cold boot); nobody kills it while the lock is held. Never run `adb logcat -c`; filter by `--pid`. Pass `-s <serial>` on every `adb` call.
- Every emulator or desktop number is labelled with its device and build mode, and none decides pass/fail.
